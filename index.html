Your George studio.

Colour Lab Hair Studio is a welcoming hair salon at 25 Courtenay Street in George, where beautiful colour and a personal touch go hand in hand.

Led by owner Stefan Ellis, known as Stef, our work spans balayage, blondes, colour corrections, cutting and hair treatments. Come as you are. Let’s explore what’s next.

Hair services in George.

01

Balayage & dimensional colour

From subtle brunette ribbons to brighter blonde looks, explore colour with depth, movement and a soft blend.

02

Blondes, foils & toning

Lighter, dimensional colour with blonde and foil work. Speak to our team about your ideal shade and how to maintain it.

03

Colour corrections

Unwanted tones or previous colour? Discuss your hair history and the look you have in mind so we can explore a correction plan.

Hair colour

Brazilian keratin treatment
